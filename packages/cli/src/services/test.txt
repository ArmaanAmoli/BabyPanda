Giant pandas are rare black-and-white bears that live in the high mountain forests of southwest China. They have a thick fur coat with black patches on their ears, legs, and around their eyes. Their diet consists almost entirely of bamboo, which makes up about 99 percent of what they eat. Because bamboo lacks high nutrition, pandas spend up to 12 hours a day eating large amounts of it. They use a special enlarged wrist bone that acts like a thumb to grab and hold bamboo stalks. Today, these beloved animals are vulnerable and protected as a national treasure due to habitat loss and small wild populations.
